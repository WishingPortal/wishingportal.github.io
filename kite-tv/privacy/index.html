Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026

Kite TV is a player for content sources you choose. The current app has no account registration, advertising or third-party usage analytics. Your saved sources and viewing preferences are not uploaded to a Kite TV backend.

Who we are

Kite TV (Android package com.wishingportal.tvplayer) is provided by Wishing Portal Ltd. For privacy questions or requests, email contact@wishingportal.com.

Information used on your device

The app stores the information needed to load your sources and remember your choices in its private Android storage. This includes:

  • Source names and addresses you enter, including any authentication details contained in those addresses.
  • Cached playlists, channel information and programme guide data from your sources.
  • Favourites, selected sources and channels, playback choices and app preferences.

The app also processes playback and connection information to play media and display local troubleshooting details. It does not automatically send these details to us or to an analytics service.

Connections to other services

When you load or refresh a source, fetch guide information or images, or play a stream, your device connects to the relevant servers. These include servers referenced by your chosen sources and any servers to which they redirect requests. They receive your IP address, the requested address, ordinary connection information and any authentication information required by the request.

These services operate independently of Kite TV and handle requests under their own privacy policies. We do not control their logging or retention. The app does not provide content sources; use only sources you have permission to access.

Storage, backup and security

Android restricts ordinary access by other apps to Kite TV's private storage. This is not a promise of additional app-level encryption. HTTPS connections are encrypted in transit; the app also accepts HTTP sources, whose requests and content are not encrypted in transit.

Android backup is enabled in the current app. Depending on your device and settings, saved source addresses, credentials within them, preferences and database data may be included in operating-system backups or device transfers. Cached playlist files are stored in a directory excluded from Android backup. Backup services are governed by your device and backup provider's settings and privacy terms.

Retention and deletion

Saved settings remain until you change or remove them, or clear the app's data. You can edit or remove saved sources in the app. Cached information may remain until replaced or cleared. To remove all local Kite TV data, use Android's Settings → Apps → Kite TV → Storage → Clear data; the exact labels vary by device. Uninstalling also removes the app's local data.

Clearing local data does not necessarily delete operating-system backups or records held by your chosen source services. Manage backups through your device or backup provider, and contact source services directly about their records.

Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any information you choose to include. We use this information to respond and resolve your request. Our email provider processes correspondence to deliver this service. We retain correspondence only as needed to handle the request and meet applicable legal obligations. Please do not send passwords, access tokens or private source addresses.

We do not sell personal information or use support correspondence for advertising. Where applicable, we process support correspondence to fulfil our legitimate interest in responding to requests and maintaining the app, and to comply with legal obligations.

Your privacy rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct or erase personal information we hold, restrict or object to processing, or request portability. Contact us using the email above to exercise these rights. We cannot access data that remains on your device. You may also complain to your local data protection authority, including the UK Information Commissioner's Office.

This webpage

This privacy page does not include analytics scripts, advertising, external fonts or cookies added by us. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. Hosting and network providers may process technical request information, such as your IP address, to deliver and secure the page. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the app's data practices change and revise the date above. Material changes will be communicated as appropriate, with consent requested where required.
